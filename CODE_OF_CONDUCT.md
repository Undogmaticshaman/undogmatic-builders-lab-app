# Code of Conduct

Builders Lab welcomes beginners, students, creators, and experienced contributors. Treat every person with respect.

We expect constructive feedback, patience with questions, credit for others' work, and discussion focused on the project. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, or sharing another person's private information is not acceptable.

If you experience or witness a problem, report it privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, moderate discussions, or restrict participation to keep the space safe and useful.
